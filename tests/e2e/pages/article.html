The bridge at the end of the road

The old house stood at the end of a long road, where the river ran under a stone bridge and the road climbed towards the mountain. In the morning a woman came down from the village with bread and salt, and the young king rode past on a black horse without a word.

The water was cold that year. A bird sang in the green field beyond the wall, and the light on the water moved like something alive. Her brother carried a heavy book under one arm and would not say what was written in it.

By evening the wind had turned. The sun went down behind the forest and the whole world smelled of rain. A dog barked somewhere near the church, and then the night came on and nobody spoke again until the morning.

The cat sat.
